What you get

Small, sharp, and out of your way in Chrome's side panel.

📝

Per-tab notes

Your note follows the page, so it survives closing the tab and restoring it later. One line is enough.

🔍

Search your own words

Search across every note, title, and tag. Type what you remember, not what the page was called.

📁

Saved sessions

Snapshot every tab in the window, restore it later. Trim the list or add a tab to it any time.

★

Star what matters

Pin the notes you keep coming back to, and jump straight to the page with one click.

🔒

Private by design

No server, no account, no analytics. Everything is stored inside your browser profile.

⚡

No sign-up wall

Install and start writing. There is nothing to create, confirm, or log into.
